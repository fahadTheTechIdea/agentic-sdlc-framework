## Research findings to preserve

- Apple distinguishes upload SDK/toolchain rules from minimum deployment targets;
  privacy/SDK/account/content/review requirements are conditional and evidence-based.
- Google distinguishes submission target APIs, form factors and existing-app
  availability. Native-library compatibility, upcoming enforcement dates and
  personal-account testing cannot be reduced to a universal platform checkbox.
- Steam's official onboarding and Steam Direct pages disagree on 21 versus 30 days.
  Keep this conflict unresolved until actual account or authoritative evidence
  establishes eligibility; conservative planning is not a compliance result.
- Policy snapshots age. Live source/console checks before each relevant submission
  remain mandatory; no automatic scheduled monitoring or scraper is installed.
